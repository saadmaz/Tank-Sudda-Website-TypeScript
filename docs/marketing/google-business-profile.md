## Business description (max 750 characters)

> Tank Sudda provides professional water tank and sump cleaning for homes, apartment buildings
> and organisations in Sri Lanka. Water tanks and underground sumps quietly collect sediment and
> build-up that you cannot see. We drain and clean overhead tanks, remove settled sediment rather
> than flushing it into your pipes, clean underground sumps, and plan larger jobs for apartment
> buildings, offices, schools and hotels. You receive a clear quotation before any work starts,
> and we tell you in advance how long your water will be off. Request a booking online or
> message us on WhatsApp with your town and tank details.
