## Quick replies

Type `/` in a chat to use these.

| Shortcut   | Message                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/details` | To give you an accurate quotation, please send your town, the tank type (plastic overhead, concrete or underground sump), its approximate size and how many tanks you have. A photo of the tank and how it is accessed helps too.                         |
| `/price`   | Our price depends on the tank size, number of tanks, access and condition. Send us those details (or a photo) and we will reply with a clear quotation before any work is booked.                                                                         |
| `/prepare` | Before the cleaning: store some water for drinking and essential use, make sure we can reach the tank or sump safely, and tell us where the main valve and pump switch are. Full guide: https://tanksudda.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-water-tank-cleaning |
| `/sump`    | If your overhead tank is filled from an underground sump, we recommend cleaning both on the same visit. Otherwise sediment in the sump can be pumped straight back into the clean tank.                                                                   |
| `/area`    | Please send your town and address and we will confirm whether we can serve your location before arranging anything.                                                                                                                                       |
| `/booked`  | Your cleaning is confirmed for [date] at [time]. Please store some water for essential use and make sure the tank can be reached. We will message you before we arrive.                                                                                   |
| `/thanks`  | Thank you for choosing Tank Sudda! If you were happy with our work, a short Google review would really help us: [review link]                                                                                                                             |
