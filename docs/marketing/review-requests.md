# Asking for Google reviews

Send this on WhatsApp **the same day** the job is finished, while the customer is happy and
remembers you. Replace `[review link]` with your Google "Ask for reviews" link (Google Business
Profile → Ask for reviews → copy link).

Rules: ask every customer the same way, never offer discounts or gifts for reviews, and never
write reviews yourself. Google can remove reviews that break these rules.

> The Sinhala and Tamil versions are draft translations. Have a native speaker check them before
> use.

## English

> Hello [name], thank you for choosing Tank Sudda today! 🙏
>
> If you were happy with the cleaning, would you mind leaving us a short Google review? It takes
> less than a minute and really helps other families find us:
> [review link]
>
> Thank you!

**Follow-up** (only once, 3 days later, if there is no review):

> Hello [name], just a gentle reminder in case you have a minute for a quick review of your tank
> cleaning: [review link]. Thank you again for choosing Tank Sudda.

## Replying to reviews

Reply to every review within a few days.

**Positive review:**

> Thank you, [name]! We are glad your tank is clean again. See you at the next cleaning. – Tank
> Sudda

**Critical review** (stay calm, take it offline):

> Thank you for your feedback, [name]. We are sorry this did not meet your expectations. Please
> contact us on +94 74 100 4296 so we can understand what happened and put it right. – Tank Sudda
