### Who we are

Tank Sudda cleans the water tanks and sumps that buildings depend on every day. We work with
[apartment buildings, offices, schools, hotels, factories — keep only those you have worked
with or want to target], and plan every job around the people who use the building.

### What we do

- **Deep cleaning** of overhead and ground-level tanks: drained, sediment removed, internal
  surfaces scrubbed and rinsed, then refilled.
- **Sump cleaning** of underground and ground-level sumps, with the pump isolated and restored.
- **Planned cleaning for whole buildings**: every tank and sump surveyed, scoped and scheduled.

### How we work with organisations

1. **Site assessment:** we survey each tank and sump, noting capacity, access and condition.
2. **Written scope and quotation:** you know exactly what is included before anything is booked.
3. **Agreed schedule:** dates and times planned with you, including notice for residents, staff
   or guests.
4. **Cleaning in an agreed order:** so part of the supply can stay available where the system
   allows it.
5. **Close-out summary:** what was cleaned, and any defects we noticed (damaged covers, leaks,
   corroded fittings).
