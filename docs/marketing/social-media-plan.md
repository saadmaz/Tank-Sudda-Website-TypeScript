# Social media plan: first month

<!-- nav -->

<sub><a href="../../README.md">Project README</a> · <a href="../README.md">Documentation index</a></sub>

Three posts a week across Facebook and Instagram (and short videos on TikTok and YouTube Shorts
when you have them). Use your own photos and videos only. Every post ends with a way to act:
the website or WhatsApp.

**Best performing content for a cleaning business:** before/after photos, short "behind the
scenes" clips of the work, and simple tips. Post at times your customers use their phones
(evenings and weekends usually work well; check your page insights after a few weeks).

**Hashtags** (pick 3–5 per post): #TankSudda #WaterTankCleaning #SumpCleaning #SriLanka
#Colombo #HomeCare #CleanWater _(add your confirmed town names)_

## Week 1: introduce Tank Sudda

1. **Who we are** _(photo: team or van with branding)_
   > Meet Tank Sudda 👋 We clean water tanks and underground sumps for homes, apartments and
   > organisations in Sri Lanka. Clear quotation before any work starts. Message us on WhatsApp
   > or book at tanksudda.com/book
2. **Tip: the hidden layer** _(photo: inside of a dirty tank floor, your own)_
   > What's at the bottom of your water tank? 🤔 Most tanks slowly collect sediment that you
   > never see. Here's what it is and why it matters: tanksudda.com/blog/understanding-sediment-in-water-tanks
3. **Before and after** _(real pair of the same tank)_
   > Before ➡️ After. A deep clean in [town]. Swipe to see the difference. Book yours:
   > tanksudda.com/book

## Week 2: sumps

4. **Do you have a sump?** _(photo: sump cover)_
   > If your water goes through an underground sump before the overhead tank, the sump needs
   > cleaning too, or sediment gets pumped straight back up. tanksudda.com/services/sump-cleaning
5. **Short video: sump clean in progress** _(15–30 seconds)_
   > Cleaning a sump in [town] today. Enclosed spaces need the right equipment, so please never
   > climb into a sump yourself.
6. **Customer question of the week**
   > "How long will my water be off?" It depends on the tank's size and condition, and we always
   > tell you in advance so you can store water. Have a question? Send it on WhatsApp.

## Week 3: preparation and trust

7. **How to prepare** _(carousel: 4 simple slides)_
   > Getting your tank cleaned? 4 things to do the day before: store water, clear the access,
   > find the main valve, know where the pump switch is. Full guide:
   > tanksudda.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-water-tank-cleaning
8. **Behind the scenes: our equipment** _(photo/video)_
   > The tools of the job 🧰 Settled sediment is lifted out of the tank, not flushed into your
   > pipes.
9. **Review** _(only a real review, with the customer's permission)_
   > Thank you, [first name], for the kind words! ⭐

## Week 4: buildings and maintenance

10. **For apartment buildings** _(photo: rooftop tanks)_
    > Managing an apartment building? We survey every tank and sump, give you a written scope,
    > and schedule cleaning around your residents. Message us for a site assessment.
11. **Maintenance checklist** _(carousel)_
    > 5 checks you can do from outside your tank this month: the lid, leaks, the water at the tap,
    > the overflow screen and the sump cover. tanksudda.com/blog/water-tank-maintenance-checklist
12. **Book before [season/holiday]**
    > [Monsoon / New Year] is coming. Book your tank clean early: tanksudda.com/book

## Simple ad test (optional)

- **Budget:** a small daily amount for 7–10 days to start
- **Audience:** your confirmed towns, ages 28–65, interests in home ownership / property
  management
- **Creative:** your best real before/after photo
- **Button:** "Send WhatsApp message" or link to tanksudda.com/services/water-tank-deep-cleaning
- **Measure:** WhatsApp chats started and bookings, not likes
